## The Problem

Building on the Holepunch stack — Pear Runtime, Bare, Hypercore, Hyperswarm,
and the surrounding ecosystem — means working with technology that most AI
tools have little to no training data on. When I started integrating these
protocols into Society, Claude could help with React, Node, and general
architecture, but would hallucinate API signatures and confuse Holepunch
primitives with unrelated libraries.

I needed a way to give Claude accurate, up-to-date knowledge about this stack.
