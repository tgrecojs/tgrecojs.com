When I'm not speaking code, I like to spend time outdoors. Being active makes me happy. I love to go out in nature, go on hikes, take in and appreciate the beauty of this world, and at night, I love to appreciate the beauty of the night sky. The [Big Dipper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Dipper), [Orion's Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion%27s_Belt), UFOs[^UFOs] (!?)... It's all so wonderful.

So when I saw a Celestron AstroMaster 70EQ for sale on Facebook Marketplace, I jumped on the opportunity, thus marking the start of my celestial exploration. Coincidentally, I created this telescope graphic prior to my telescope purchase... talk about foreshadowing. Colorfigures is a [pretty cool project](https://april-zhu.com/colorfigures) that I highly suggest taking for a spin (literally, it spins!).

Needless to say, the telescope graphic was the first thing I reached for when putting together this site after finally getting [tgrecojs.com](https://tgrecojs.com) back in my possession...

I suppose you can say "all of the stars aligned"... 😏

Fingers crossed I will be able to share some beautiful pictures of the night sky in the near future, so if you're into that sort of thing, then stay tuned, space cadet. 🧑‍🚀

[^UFOs]: I'm aware that [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/) changed the nomenclature regarding UFOs, and now refers to them as UAPs. And I humbly reject this new verbiage.
